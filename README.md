# Asantys Energy Desk (Prototype)

A local, single-page prototype for Asantys Systems East Africa with a customer-facing solar website, an indicative KPLC-bill estimator, an assessment inquiry form, selected project references, and a browser-local sales lead workspace.

## Run locally

1. Extract `asantys_solar_platform.zip`.
2. Open the extracted `asantys_solar_platform` folder.
3. Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome or Edge).

The photos used by the website are stored in the `assets/` folder and load locally. Keep that folder beside `index.html` when moving or hosting the site.

## What is included

- Responsive homepage for residential solar PV, battery storage and hybrid KPLC systems.
- Photo-backed featured projects on the homepage.
- Solutions & projects page with eight case studies based on the supplied Asantys Systems East Africa project-reference brochure (Datasheet 04.2025).
- Indicative KPLC-bill estimator. It is a conversation aid and does not replace engineering design or a formal quote.
- Solar assessment inquiry form that creates a lead in the browser-local sales workspace.
- Lead pipeline with search, status changes, follow-up date, notes and CSV export.
- Starter research records for Karunguru Gated Community and Mara Leisure Camp / Mara Ardhi redevelopment, marked as samples.

## Reference images and project claims

The photos were extracted from the user-supplied `Selected Solar Project References – Asantys Systems East Africa` PDF and optimized for website display. Project names, specifications and reported outcomes in the gallery follow that supplied brochure. Confirm current specifications and obtain the appropriate client/photo permissions with the Asantys team before publishing or sharing externally.

## Prototype limitations

- Lead data remains in local browser storage on the current device; this is not a shared team CRM.
- The inquiry form does not send data to a server.
- There is no staff authentication, database, automatic WhatsApp/email messaging or public hosting configured.
- KPLC tariff, solar capacity, bill-offset and savings values are illustrative assumptions only. A qualified technical assessment is required before recommending or pricing a system.
